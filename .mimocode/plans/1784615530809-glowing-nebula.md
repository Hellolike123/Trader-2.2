## Phase 1: Create `ab_price_action.py`

**File:** `02-共享模块-shared/trader_shared/ab_price_action.py`

Core Al Brooks signal module. Functions:

### 1. `detect_signal_bar(bar, prev_bar, direction="bull") -> dict`
Signal bar detection (Al Brooks Ch.5-6).
- **Bull reversal bar**: close near high, tail ≥ 50% of range, close > open
- **Bear reversal bar**: close near low, tail ≥ 50% of range, close < open
- **Strong signal bar**: tail ≤ 30% of range, close at extreme (stronger)
- Returns: `{type: "strong_bull"/"weak_bull"/"strong_bear"/"weak_bear"/"none", tail_ratio, close_position}`

### 2. `check_follow_through(bars, signal_bar_idx, direction="bull") -> dict`
Follow-through confirmation (Al Brooks Ch.3, Ch.10).
- After bull signal bar: next bar closes above signal bar's high → confirmed
- After bear signal bar: next bar closes below signal bar's low → confirmed
- **Second entry**: second attempt in same direction = higher reliability
- Returns: `{confirmed: bool, entry_price, follow_through_strength, is_second_entry}`

### 3. `count_high_low_pullbacks(bars, direction="up") -> dict`
H1/H2/H3 and L1/L2/L3 counting (Al Brooks Ch.17, Ch.20).
- **H1**: first bar in pullback with high > prior bar's high (in uptrend)
- **H2**: second H1 after another pullback (more reliable entry)
- **H3**: third push — wedge旗形, reversal risk
- L1/L2/L3 mirror for downtrend
- Returns: `{count: int, label: "H1"/"H2"/"H3"/"L1"/"L2"/"L3", quality: "good"/"fair"/"weak"}`

### 4. `determine_always_in(bars) -> dict`
Always-In direction (Al Brooks Ch.15).
- Count bull trend bars vs bear trend bars in recent window (20 bars)
- Check if price is above/below EMA20
- Strong trend: >60% bars in one direction
- Returns: `{direction: "bull"/"bear"/"neutral", strength: "strong"/"moderate"/"weak", evidence}`

### 5. `detect_breakout_mode(bars) -> dict`
Breakout mode detection (Al Brooks Ch.1, Ch.3).
- **Tight trading range**: bars mostly overlapping, small bodies → breakout mode
- **Spike**: 2+ strong trend bars, little overlap → trending
- Returns: `{mode: "breakout"/"trending"/"range", range_height, overlap_ratio}`

### 6. `build_ab_signal(bars_5m, current_price) -> dict`
Main entry point — combines all above into a single signal dict.
- Calls all sub-functions
- Returns: `{direction, signal_bar, follow_through, pullback_count, always_in, breakout_mode, buy: bool, sell: bool, reason: str, buy_price, sell_price}`

**Design principles:**
- Pure functions, no state, no I/O
- Input: `bars_5m` (list of OHLCV dicts) + `current_price`
- All detection on 5m bars (T0 execution layer)
- Minimal config — hardcode Al Brooks' rules, no tunable params

## Key risks

1. **Al Brooks signal quality**: Signal bar detection is rule-based but subjective. May need tuning after initial implementation.
2. **H/L counting on 5m**: Counting relies on pullback detection quality. May produce noisy signals if pullbacks are tiny.
3. **Always-In in choppy markets**: Neutral state may fire too often in range-bound days, reducing resonance triggers.
4. **No divergence in Al Brooks**: Chan's背驰 was covered by Momentum. If Momentum divergence detection is weak, overall signal quality drops. Mitigation: verify RSI divergence works well on 5m.
